Before you come

What happens when there's lightning?

Everyone leaves the water when we see lightning or hear thunder. Pools reopen 30 minutes after the last strike or rumble. We may open late or close early for storms, rain or air under 70°F.

Do you give rain checks?

Yes, if the attractions close for more than an hour and the park closes before 4 PM. Pick up a return-visit voucher at the front gate. It's good for the rest of the season.

Can my kid ride the big slides?

Pipeline Tower and Cannonball need riders over 48″. Tropical Twister starts at 42″. The kids' cove slides are for riders under 48″, and every pool has life vests in all sizes.

What should we wear?

Swimwear in every pool. Rubber swim pants and swim diapers for little ones. Street clothes cloud the water and clog the filters.

Is the park accessible?

A concrete walkway runs through the whole park. Ask any team member at the gate about accessible entry and the pool lifts.

Can we leave and come back?

Yes. Get a hand stamp at the gate. Pavilion guests wear wristbands and skip the line, even when the park is full.
